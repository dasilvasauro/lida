New Pomodoro, clock tick and metronome

• New: Redesigned Pomodoro: you choose the minutes by turning a strip of numbers, like a kitchen timer. The center number grows and the ones at the edges shrink as if they were on a cylinder, with a vibration every minute (stronger on multiples of 5). While the Pomodoro runs, the strip turns on its own with the time.
